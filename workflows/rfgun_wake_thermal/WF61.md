# WF6.1: longitudinal-constrained low-loss search

Canonical branch: `workflow/6.1-longitudinal-heat`.

Both UpperHeight coordinates are fixed to **49.9 mm** and DownHeight coordinates
to **-47 mm**, following the user's amendment after 50 mm failed to rebuild/save.
Eight free coordinates remain: each antenna's self-angle, Lin, projection and
FolkHeight. Angles are degrees; lengths are mm; bend radius magnitude is 25 mm.
The old geometries are references, not electromagnetic observations of the new
fixed geometry. Do not claim literal outer-conductor removal from parameters alone.

Two seeds use WF6 candidate0008 and candidate0037 with the four fixed coordinates
replaced. Search within the union of neighborhoods: self-angle +/-20 degrees,
Lin +/-10 mm, projection +/-3 mm, FolkHeight +/-5 mm, intersected with the original
physical bounds. Preserve both neighborhoods in the proposal pool. The first two
candidate IDs in the new round identify these two seeds, not old numerical results.

Only axis and thermal stages are enabled. Axis uses the existing 70 mm bunch,
35 m tail and 550--1462.7348957 MHz band. Formal longitudinal acceptance requires
qualified whole-band reconstruction and its bounded envelope below **1500 ohm**;
unresolved windows remain uncertain. Finite full-band estimates still inform the
GP with existing uncertainty rules. A bounded envelope below 1500 ohm admits
thermal normally; the inherited 20% exploration budget permits some uncertain
or exceeding longitudinal cases without declaring them feasible.

Three independent GPs regress longitudinal ohm and the two component losses in W.
Acquisition seeks lower max(P5,P6) subject to longitudinal feasibility; total power
breaks report ties. Thermal scaling remains 500 kV peak, CW, beta=1. The 150 W
value is a reference/soft target, never a stopping condition or saturated objective.
Reports retain all formal feasible cases separately from screening candidates.
Native retention keeps the two seeds and up to ten lowest-loss formal cases plus
ten screening cases, subject to existing ownership/evidence checks.

Dual-station recovery, saved-result extraction, reopen/rebuild recovery and
cross-station retry are inherited from current WF6. No X/Y stages are admitted.
The round clock is **48 h shared wall time**, not 48 h per node summed together.
Reaching the cap closes admission but drains running stages; actual overrun is
recorded. Each next round requires manual user authorization. No automatic restart.

`run_workflow_6_fleet.py prepare-heat --config CONFIG --nodes NODES --reference OLD_STATE`
prepares a fresh paused round with STOP, two unobserved seeds, no jobs and no
charged time. The reference snapshot may retain old unresolved station entries;
these are not migrated. Before a later authorized start, inspect both stations,
reconcile old ownership, verify deployed execution fingerprints and remove only
the new round's STOP markers. Preparation itself launches no worker or CST.

Validation must distinguish full branch no-CST tests, live geometry-only
rebuild/save checks, and live solves. Passing the former two does not validate
mesh, working mode, field extraction or optimization quality at the new geometry.
